Sacred Mantras

Select a verse to reveal its resonance and meaning.

ஓம் ஹ்ரீம் க்லீம் சாஸ்த்ரு-ரூபாய விஷ்ணுமாயாய நம:
"Om Hreem Kleem Shasthru-Rupaya Vishnumayaya Namaha"
Meaning: The fundamental Moola Mantra invoking the divine protector who took the form of Shastha. 'Hreem' invokes the power of the soul and cosmic energy, while 'Kleem' invokes magnetic attraction and the fulfillment of prayers through the grace of Lord Vishnumaya.
ஓம் பூத நாதாய வித்மஹே
மஹிஷாரூடாய தீமஹி
தன்னோ மாய ப்ரசோதயாத்
"Om Bhootha Nadhaya Vidmahe
Mahisharoodaya Dheemahi
Tanno Maya Prachodayat"
Meaning: Om. Let us meditate on the Lord of all Beings (Bhootha Nadha). May He who rides the Buffalo (Mahisha) inspire us. May that Lord of Maya illuminate our intellect.
நிஜ புஜ நிஹித குருவடி தண்டம்
மஹிஷாரூடம் மஹா-பலம் |
சாஸ்த்ரு-ரூபம் ஸ்யாமவர்ணம்
விஷ்ணுமாயாம் நமாம்யஹம் ||
"Nija Bhuja Nihita Kuruvadi Dandam
Mahisharoodam Maha-Balam |
Shasthru-Rupam Syamavarnam
Vishnumayam Namamyaham ||"
Meaning: I bow to Lord Vishnumaya, who holds the sacred Kuruvadi (magic wand) in his own hand. He is the possessor of great strength who rides upon the buffalo. I salute the dark-complexioned Lord who appeared in the form of Shastha.
